85%

SECTION 7

7. Artist Payouts

Artist earnings accumulate in real-time inside the Artist Studio Dashboard. Payouts are remitted directly via automated NIP (NIBSS Instant Payment) to any licensed Commercial Bank, Merchant Bank, or CBN-approved Mobile Money Operator (MMO) in Nigeria.

  • Minimum Threshold: The minimum withdrawable balance is ₦2,000 (Two Thousand Nigerian Naira).
  • Settlement Schedule: Standard withdrawals clear within twenty-four (24) to forty-eight (48) banking hours.
  • Hold Period: Newly generated earnings may undergo a 72-hour fraud prevention cooling period before release to prevent chargeback anomalies.
