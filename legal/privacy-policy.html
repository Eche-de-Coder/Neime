STATUTORY ACCREDITATION NDPA 2023

Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) Compliant

Neime is registered and functions strictly within the parameters established by the Nigeria Data Protection Act of 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). We uphold stringent data privacy benchmarks tailored to Africa's creator economy.

01

1. Introduction

Welcome to Neime ("we", "us", "our"), the digital stage empowering artists, listeners, and creators across Nigeria and globally. Neime operates as an independent music streaming, direct fan monetization, and copyright distribution platform. We respect the autonomy, creativity, and privacy of every listener and creator on our stage.

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Neime mobile applications, desktop experience, application programming interfaces (APIs), and related digital services (collectively, the "Platform"). It clarifies what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how your Naira-denominated transactions and audio streaming interactions are handled, and the measures we employ under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) and international best practices to ensure absolute confidentiality and control.

02

2. Information We Collect

In operating our rhythmic ecosystem, we systematically gather specific classifications of data to provide audio playback, process royalty distributions, and secure intellectual property:

badge

2.1 Account & Profile Data

Full legal name, stage name/pseudonym, email address, phone number, encrypted password, profile biography, display avatar, and user category (e.g., Listener, Verified Artist, Producer).

mic

2.2 Artist & Catalog Data

Audio recordings, ISRC and ISWC metadata codes, songwriting credits, release artworks, performance rights organization (PRO) affiliations, and verified government identification for copyright claim verification.

shopping_cart

2.3 Purchase & Subscription Data

Transaction history, track purchases, premium streaming subscription tier, crowdfunding pledge histories, timestamps, billing addresses, and tokenized payment gateway tokens.

account_balance

2.4 Payout & Banking Details

For monetizing creators: Nigerian commercial bank account numbers (NUBAN), routing information, Bank Verification Numbers (BVN) validated strictly via NDPC-licensed identity partners, and Tax Identification Numbers (TIN).

devices

2.5 Technical & Stream Analytics

IP addresses, hardware identifier, operating system version, browser classification, listen count, skip rates, repeat frequency, offline cache status, and mobile network telemetrics.

hub

2.6 & 2.7 Communications & Third Parties

Support correspondence, fan direct messages, feedback notes, and integrated single-sign-on (SSO) profile insights derived from platforms such as Apple, Google, or Spotify.

Neime uses the collected information strictly for designated purposes that sustain the platform and protect our artistic collective:

  • check_circle Curated Audio Delivery: Powering algorithmic vibe feeds, queue generation, high-fidelity caching, and custom playlists tailored to individual tastes.
  • check_circle Financial Payouts & Direct Support: Calculating accurate stream revenue shares, routing Naira payments to artist NUBAN accounts, and printing digital transaction receipts.

  • check_circle Customer Support & Safety: Investigating reported tracks, resolving account compromise claims, and maintaining community standards.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Under Section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, every operation performed on your personal data is backed by one of the following recognized legal foundations:

BASE 1

Contractual Necessity

Processing required to perform our terms of service, maintain account sessions, and remit royalties to artists.

BASE 2

Legal Obligation

Mandatory financial record-keeping under Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) and anti-money laundering (AML) laws.

BASE 3

Legitimate Interests

Protecting our systems from automated streaming bots, identity theft, and improving overall app telemetry.

BASE 4

Consent

Explicit, unbundled permission requested before delivering push campaigns or executing optional location features.

5. Music, Profiles, and Public Information

Neime is a communal sound experience. By default, certain information you submit is made accessible to all listeners across the digital stage:

• Public Artist Profiles: Artist stage names, cover art, track catalogs, public bios, social handles, and verification badges.

• Community Activity: Public playlist names, collaborator track credits, and comments left on live premiere releases.

• Privacy Controls: Listeners may toggle their streaming activity, recently played records, and follower counts to "Private" directly inside Account Preferences.

06

6. How We Share Personal Information

We never sell user personal data. We disclose information only under strict confidentiality and encryption protocols to:

Licensed Payment Processors

Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)-licensed payment switches (e.g., Paystack, Flutterwave) for safe debit/credit card and direct bank transfer checkouts.

Cloud & Content Delivery Partners

High-speed Edge audio distribution networks and secure ISO-certified server infrastructure.

Copyright & Royalty Collective Societies

Statutory licensing boards and collective management organizations (CMOs) for statutory rights reconciliation.

Law Enforcement & Legal Precepts

When formally compelled by valid Nigerian court orders or enforceable regulatory summonses.

07

Marketing & Comms

We send updates on new drops, exclusive listening rooms, and platform changes. You can revoke newsletter and marketing notification consents at any point via the 1-click unsubscribe link or push notification settings.

08

Cookies & Local Storage

We utilize essential session tokens, browser cookies, and local database storage to remember audio volume, offline encrypted playback files, and secure authentication tokens without tracking you across third-party websites.

09

Data Security

All data transmissions are guarded by TLS 1.3 encryption. Banking details are tokenized and stored in PCI-DSS Tier 1 certified vaults with continuous vulnerability evaluations and strict multi-factor role privileges.

10

Data Retention

Account data is maintained while your account remains open. Upon closure, personal identifiers are purged or anonymized within 90 days, except financial logs held up to 7 years to fulfill Nigerian statutory taxation rules.

11

11. International Data Transfers

While Neime originates in Nigeria, our audio delivery infrastructure spans a global audience. Whenever personal data is transferred across national boundaries, we enforce Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and verify that the destination jurisdiction guarantees adequate protections as mandated by Section 41–43 of the NDPA 2023.

14. Children & Young Users

Neime is not directed toward individuals under the age of 13. Users between 13 and 17 must possess parental or guardian authorization to activate monetization payouts or make track purchases. We promptly remove records linked to unauthorized minors upon detection.
